    The rogue in Forever

    Rogue

    Stealth, poisons and picking your moment. Blizzard has not published its rogue changes yet, so this page is short.

    Blizzard describes rogues as relying on "a blend of stealth and minor mysticism", and adds that a rogue's allegiance "lasts only as long as the latest contract". Almost anyone can be one: every race in the game except the tauren.

    One change that applies to everybody helps here. Hit chance is now a single stat, and Blizzard says that makes tools such as poisons more reliable.

    Who can be one

    Alliance: Dwarf, gnome, human, night elf, High Order Skyborne.
    Horde: Orc, troll, undead, Windshaper Skyborne.

    What we don't know yet

    There is no Blizzard deep dive for the rogue yet. We don't know what has changed in its abilities or talents, and we will fill this in when Blizzard says.

    New here?

    Stealth means enemies can't see you until you strike. A rogue chooses when a fight starts, which no other class can do as well.
