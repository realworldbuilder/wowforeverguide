    The warrior in Forever

    Warrior

    The armoured fighter at the front, and the one class every race on both factions can play.

    A warrior either tanks, holding the enemy's attention and taking the hits, or deals damage. Blizzard's summary is that any party or raid is happy to have one in either role, and that warriors lack the tools to recover easily from taking too much damage. They need a healer more than most.

    Everything a warrior does runs on Rage, which builds as you hit and get hit. Most of Forever's changes are about making that more dependable.

    Who can be one

    Alliance: Dwarf, gnome, human, night elf, High Order Skyborne.
    Horde: Orc, tauren, troll, undead, Windshaper Skyborne.

    What Blizzard has changed

    • Armor no longer reduces the Rage you gain from taking damage.
    • Rage from dealing damage is based on weapon speed, with more from critical strikes.
    • Victory Rush, learned at level 20, heals you for 10% of your health after a kill.
    • Recklessness, Retaliation and Shield Wall no longer share a cooldown.
    • A Protection talent lets you Charge while in Defensive Stance.
    New here?

    Tanking is the job of being the one the monsters hit. It's the role that sets the pace for a group, which is why warriors tend to end up leading.
