    The priest in Forever

    Priest

    Healing, protection and shadow damage. In Forever every priest gets tools that used to belong to one race.

    Priests are the healers most groups look for first, and they can also turn to shadow magic and deal damage instead. Blizzard's changes keep that shape and widen who gets what.

    Each race of priest still has two spells of its own. Gnome priests, new in Forever, get Confounding Flash, which confuses up to five nearby enemies, and Contingency Plan, a ward that shields and heals a friend whose health drops low.

    Who can be one

    Alliance: Dwarf, gnome, human, night elf.
    Horde: Troll, undead.

    What Blizzard has changed

    • Fear Ward and Devouring Plague are learned by every priest at level 20, whatever their race.
    • Divine Spirit is learned by every priest at level 30.
    • Shadow Word: Death is a new spell at level 32.
    • Prayer of Healing heals the target's party rather than only the priest's own.
    New here?

    A healer keeps the group alive. It's the role groups are most often short of, so a priest rarely waits long for an invitation.
