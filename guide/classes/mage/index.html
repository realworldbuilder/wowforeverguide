    The mage in Forever

    Mage

    Frost and flame from a distance. Blizzard has not published its mage changes yet, so this page is short.

    Blizzard's description of the mage is a frail wizard who would rather not be hit, "hurling powerful bolts of frost and flame" from range. Mages also bring Polymorph, which takes one enemy out of a fight for a while. Blizzard has said that kind of crowd control still has a place in Forever's dungeons.

    Pick it if you like standing well back and seeing large numbers.

    Who can be one

    Alliance: Gnome, human, High Order Skyborne.
    Horde: Orc, troll, undead.

    What we don't know yet

    There is no Blizzard deep dive for the mage yet. We don't know what has changed in its spells or talents, and we will fill this in when Blizzard says.

    New here?

    Crowd control means switching an enemy off for a few seconds so the group can deal with the others first. Mages are the best known for it.
