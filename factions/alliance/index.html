    Before you roll

    The Alliance in Forever

    Four old nations and one new arrival. In Forever the Alliance can field all nine classes for the first time, because dwarves can now be shaman.

    Blizzard describes the Alliance as "bound together by proud traditions of nobility, honor, faith, justice, and sacrifice".

    Three things are new on this side. Humans can be hunters, gnomes can be priests and dwarves can be shaman. The third matters most, because it gives the Alliance a class it has never had.

    The fifth race is new altogether. The High Order Skyborne are, in Blizzard's words, descendants of ancient scholars who have been called back to Azeroth to recover the knowledge they lost. The Skyborne exist on both factions, and you choose a side when you make one.

    The five races

    Human

    The all-rounders, with seven of the nine classes.

    Classes: Hunter, mage, paladin, priest, rogue, warlock, warrior. New in Forever: Hunter.
    Racial traits: Will to Survive, Perception, Sword Specialization, The Human Spirit.

    Dwarf

    The only Alliance race that can be a shaman.

    Classes: Hunter, paladin, priest, rogue, shaman, warrior. New in Forever: Shaman.
    Racial traits: Stoneform, Find Treasure, Mace Specialization, Big Game Hunter.

    Gnome

    Gnome priests get two spells no other priest has.

    Classes: Mage, priest, rogue, warlock, warrior. New in Forever: Priest.
    Racial traits: Escape Artist, Eureka!, Expansive Mind, Engineering Specialization.

    Night Elf

    One of two Alliance races that can be druids.

    Classes: Druid, hunter, priest, rogue, warrior.
    Racial traits: Elune's Light, Shadowmeld, Quickness, Wisp Spirit.

    High Order Skyborne

    The new race. Alliance Skyborne can be mages; Horde Skyborne cannot.

    Classes: Druid, hunter, mage, rogue, warrior. New in Forever: The whole race.
    Racial traits: Walk on Air, Read Ley Line, Wind Blessed, Elemental Insight.

    More on the Skyborne: both versions, what the traits do and how to get one

    Every race now has four racial traits, two you press and two that are always on. They are listed with each race above. Blizzard says several were reworked for Forever.

    New here?

    Your faction decides who you can play with. Blizzard has said Horde and Alliance players still cannot group together in Forever, so choose the side your friends are on first and the race second.
