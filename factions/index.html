    Before you roll

    Alliance or Horde

    The first choice you make, and the one that decides who you can play with. Each side has five races in Forever, and for the first time each side can field all nine classes.

    • The Alliance in Forever

      Four old nations and one new arrival. In Forever the Alliance can field all nine classes for the first time, because dwarves can now be shaman.

    • The Horde in Forever

      Four old nations and one new arrival. In Forever the Horde can field all nine classes for the first time, because the undead can now be paladins.
