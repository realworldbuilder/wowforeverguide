    Testing in beta

    Pets never stop eating

    2004 said

    A happy pet does 125% damage. An unhappy one does 75% and starts to lose its loyalty.

    Forever says

    Blizzard hasn't said whether pet happiness returns. We're testing it in the beta.

    The hunter chapter gives pet happiness three levels: happy at 125% damage, content at 100% and unhappy at 75%. Let it stay unhappy and the pet thinks about leaving. There is a warning box about how much a pet eats.

    Blizzard's hunter post for Forever doesn't mention happiness at all. Rambleon is checking it in the beta, and this entry will be updated with what we find.

    New here?

    A hunter's pet is an animal you tame in the wild. It fights beside you and keeps the enemy busy while you shoot.
