the zone order, 1 to 60

every zone you can level in on WoW Forever, both factions, in the order you'll reach them. the number on the left is the level range; under each zone is where you can fly to and the dungeon you'll find there.

tip two zones at the same level? do the one with your flight point first.red means no flight point for your side: walk in.
